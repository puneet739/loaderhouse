Collect delivery proof against the right load

Proof of delivery is most useful when it is tied to the exact shipment, receiver, transporter, and delivery time. Loaderhouse is designed to help teams collect POD files, notes, and completion details against the load record so operations and finance teams do not have to search across chats later.

Close the gap between delivery and billing

Missing delivery documents can slow customer updates, billing checks, transporter settlement, and internal reporting. Loaderhouse keeps proof collection visible as a pending action until the record is complete. That makes it easier for dispatchers to follow up while the delivery is still fresh and easier to verify.

Handle real-world document quality issues

POD records are not always perfect. Images may be unclear, stamps may be missing, receiver remarks may need review, or a document may arrive late. Loaderhouse can support status notes and review flags so teams know whether proof is accepted, pending, or needs another follow-up.

Link delivery proof with tracking history

Delivery proof should complete the movement story. When connected with booking and tracking, the POD record shows how the load was assigned, when it moved, what happened during delivery, and how closure was confirmed. This helps teams answer customer questions and review exceptions without rebuilding the timeline manually.

Frequently asked questions

What is proof of delivery management?

It is the process of collecting, storing, reviewing, and linking delivery confirmation documents to the correct shipment record.

What documents can count as proof of delivery?

Depending on the business process, proof may include signed challans, stamped invoices, delivery notes, images, or receiver acknowledgements.

Can Loaderhouse reduce missing POD follow-ups?

The planned workflow keeps POD status visible so teams can see which shipments still need documents or review.

Does proof of delivery replace shipment tracking?

No. Tracking shows movement progress, while proof of delivery confirms that the shipment reached the receiver and was closed.

Who uses POD records after delivery?

Operations, customer support, finance, sales, and transporter coordination teams may all need POD records for closure and dispute checks.
