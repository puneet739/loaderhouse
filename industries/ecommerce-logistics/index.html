Frequently asked questions

What does ecommerce logistics include?

Ecommerce logistics includes pickup planning, warehouse movement, customer delivery, returns, shipment tracking, proof of delivery, and coordination with transport partners.

Can Loaderhouse support both first-mile and last-mile movement?

Loaderhouse is designed to support planned goods movement across pickup, transfer, and delivery workflows. Exact service availability depends on the route, shipment type, and operating coverage.

How does visibility help ecommerce teams?

Visibility helps teams know whether a shipment is booked, picked up, in transit, delayed, delivered, or facing an exception, which reduces repeated calls between operations, support, and transport partners.

Is ecommerce logistics only for small parcels?

No. Ecommerce logistics can include parcels, cartons, inventory transfers, bulk marketplace movement, return loads, and larger shipments that need suitable vehicle capacity.

What should ecommerce teams share before booking transport?

Teams should share pickup and delivery locations, shipment weight and volume, package count, delivery timelines, handling needs, invoice or e-way bill details where applicable, and contact information for both ends.
