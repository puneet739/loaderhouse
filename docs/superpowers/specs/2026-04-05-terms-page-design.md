# Terms Page Design

## Summary

Create a static terms of use page for Loaderhouse that is published at `/terms` on GitHub Pages. The page will set stronger, platform-facing terms for Indian operations by `N & M KANYSHA LOGISTICS LLP` operating under the `Loaderhouse` brand.

## Goals

- Make terms available at `https://www.loaderhouse.com/terms`.
- Cover launch-stage logistics platform use in India with stronger operational and liability protections.
- Reuse the established long-form legal page styling from the privacy policy.
- Update the homepage footer so the terms link resolves to the new route.

## Scope

- Add `terms/index.html`.
- Update the homepage footer terms link to `/terms`.
- Extend the existing route verification script to assert the new page and link.
- Include sections for eligibility, service scope, account use, bookings, prohibited shipments, pricing, cancellations, third-party services, IP, disclaimers, liability limits, indemnity, suspension, governing law, and contact details.

## Constraints

- The repo is a plain static site served by GitHub Pages.
- The terms must be written for Indian operations only.
- The page must identify `N & M KANYSHA LOGISTICS LLP` as the operator and `Loaderhouse` as the brand.
- The drafting should be conservative and protective without inventing operational commitments not present on the site.

## Notes

- These are website and platform-facing terms, not a negotiated master services agreement or carriage contract.
- Stronger protective language should be included around prohibited items, user accuracy, liability caps, delays, service availability, and claims timing.
